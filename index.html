OUR STORY

From Strike Crew to Coordinators

The Foundation — Strike Crew Experts

We started where most don't want to — the heavy lifting. Setup and tear down. Delivering items in safe condition, transporting to venues, loading, and assisting in creation. We were the extension of coordinators, planners, florists — the hands that turned small ideas into massive creations.

The Growth — 100+ Events Strong

From intimate gatherings to massive productions, in-state and out. We worked with the biggest names and learned from the best AND the worst. Every event taught us what separates a good coordinator from a great one.

The Evolution — Ready to Lead

We've seen every angle of this industry. Now we're stepping into the coordinator role — combining our on-the-ground execution expertise with the strategic vision to run events that exceed expectations. We don't just follow plans. We elevate them.
